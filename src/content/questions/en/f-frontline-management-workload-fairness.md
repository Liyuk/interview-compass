question: A member says the workload is too heavy and nothing can be adjusted. How do you assess their workload reasonably? If you suspect exaggeration or even deception, how do you verify it without damaging trust?
intent: See whether you can assess workload with facts and stay fair between caring about a member's feelings and guarding against exaggeration.
signals:
  - Assesses with concrete items, time, and priorities rather than feel or impression
  - Separates real overload, an efficiency problem, and exaggeration to avoid certain work
  - Verifies with respect, treating the claim as possibly true first and then checking it with facts
  - Whatever the conclusion, gives a concrete next step, such as changing priorities, adding support, or setting clear expectations
