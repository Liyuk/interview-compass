title: How do you influence a peer team to reprioritize when you have no reporting authority?
question: >-
  Your project depends on another team fixing a data defect. They have higher-priority work and no reporting
  relationship with you, but customers cannot reconcile accounts. How would you make the case and offer decision
  evidence?
intent: >-
  Assess whether you use shared goals, impact evidence, options, and a clear request to influence without
  authority.
signals:
  - State customer impact, scope, timing, and cost of delay.
  - Understand the other team’s goals and commitments without personalizing the conflict.
  - Offer scope reduction, resource exchange, or staged delivery options.
