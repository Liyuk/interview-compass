title: Did you ever try to break out of your resource or collaboration limits?
question: Did you ever try to break out of your resource or collaboration limits, such as bringing other teams in or asking upward for larger resources? How did you make the case, and what happened?
intent: See whether you accept limits passively or argue for more with evidence, and whether you know what to do when the attempt fails.
signals:
  - Says what was actually tried, not only that it was considered
  - Makes the case for resources with facts and expected results, not only a statement of need
  - When resources were not granted, says how the commitment was narrowed or the scope adjusted
  - For limits it did not push on, can explain why not
