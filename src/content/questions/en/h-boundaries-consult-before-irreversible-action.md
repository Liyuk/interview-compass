title: What do you do first when an out-of-scope action may be irreversible?
question: >-
  On call, you find a data-repair script that could quickly restore a report but might overwrite changes
  customers just submitted. The owner is unreachable and the business asks you to act immediately. How would
  you separate protective steps you can take, actions requiring authorization, and escalation?
intent: >-
  Assess whether under urgent pressure you can judge authority boundaries, control irreversible risk, and take
  protective action proportionate to your role.
signals:
  - Identifies reversibility, affected parties, data-loss risk, and current authorization
  - Takes temporary steps that do not widen harm while avoiding unauthorized irreversible writes
  - >-
    Records facts, contacts an authorized decision-maker, escalates, verifies recovery, and hands off
    responsibility
