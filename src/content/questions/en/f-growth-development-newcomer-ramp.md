  When a newcomer joins, how do you bring them from completing tasks to owning something independently? What
  do you assign in the first three months, how do you give feedback, and how do you tell when to let go?
intent: "Assess whether you design an onboarding path around the role and prior experience, gradually transfer judgment ownership, and use evidence of independent routine and exception handling to decide when to let go."
