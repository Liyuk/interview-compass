title: When a newcomer joins, how do you bring them from completing tasks to owning something independently?
question: When a newcomer joins, how do you bring them from completing tasks to owning something independently? What do you assign in the first three months, how do you give feedback, and how do you tell when to let go?
intent: See whether you design a clear ramp-up path, real problems, and steady feedback for newcomers, and know when to step back.
signals:
  - Gives a clear ramp-up path with stage goals instead of leaving the newcomer to feel their way
  - Assigns real problems rather than only exercises, and says how difficulty rises step by step
  - Says how often and in what way feedback is given, and how the newcomer is helped to voice difficulties
  - Uses concrete evidence to judge when to let go and says how it is checked afterwards
