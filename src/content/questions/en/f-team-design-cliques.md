title: Cliques have formed on the team. What do you do?
question: Cliques have formed on the team. How do you judge whether they help or hurt the team, and how do you deal with their effect on the fairness of information, resources, and decisions?
intent: See how you tell ordinary closeness from exclusive circles that harm a team, and respond with mechanisms rather than blame.
signals:
  - Separates naturally formed closeness from circles that exclude others and hoard information
  - Judges by watching information flow, how opportunities are allocated, and who takes part in decisions, not by impression
  - Uses transparent mechanisms, such as open decisions and open allocation of opportunities, to weaken the basis of unfairness
  - Talks privately with key people instead of blaming in front of the team
