  - Watches concrete signals, such as whether the lead can state goals and boundaries, whether partners know whom to go to, and whether they can make a choice in a real conflict
  - Separates individual capability, role design, delegation, and support, writes disagreements as concrete questions first, then decides whether to adjust the person, the role, or the support
