title: What is your logic for developing people? What do you focus on at each stage?
question: What is your logic for developing people? What do you focus on improving in a newcomer, a core member who delivers independently, and a team lead, and how do you tell they are ready for the next stage?
intent: See whether you have a development logic that changes with stage, rather than one approach for everyone.
signals:
  - Says what the core shift is at each stage, such as from completing tasks to independent judgment to lifting others
  - Gives concrete evidence for moving to the next stage, rather than time served or impressions
  - Says how your own role should change at each stage
  - Names the limits of the logic, such as where it does not suit everyone
