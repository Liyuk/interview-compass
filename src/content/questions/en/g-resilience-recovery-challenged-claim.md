title: An interviewer directly challenges your result, saying it was mostly luck or the team's doing. How do you respond?
question: An interviewer directly challenges your result, saying it was mostly luck or mostly the credit of others on the team. How do you respond?
intent: See how steady you are when sharply challenged, and whether you can discuss the facts without becoming emotional or backing down blindly.
signals:
  - Stays composed and first works out which point is being challenged, rather than hurrying to defend
  - Separates what the other person has a point about from what needs to be clarified with facts
  - Readily admits luck and the team's contribution while stating clearly their own judgment and actions
  - Does not attack the questioner and does not reverse the facts just because they were challenged
