title: A critical responsibility has only one capable owner
question: >-
  A critical business area depends on one owner, and handoff documents do not enable anyone else to operate it
  independently. How would you build real succession capability?
intent: >-
  Assess how you handle “A critical responsibility has only one capable owner”: clarify goals and constraints,
  make an evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - >-
    Identify critical judgments, exceptions, contacts, and permissions, distinguishing knowledge gaps
    from formal authority gaps.
  - >-
    Compare pairing, rotation, rehearsals, and staged handoffs by cost and business risk, giving
    successors real practice.
  - >-
    Verify independent handling without the former owner present rather than substituting document counts
    or a backup roster for capability.
