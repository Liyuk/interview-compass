question: >-
  When you did your research, what were the industry level, the best practice in this direction, and the level
  already reached inside your company? Where does your solution fall, and why?
intent: >-
  See whether you have layered reference points and can state the gap between your solution and each one,
  rather than only saying "we do well".
signals:
  - >-
    Separates the industry baseline, best practice in the area, and the company’s current level, with a sound
    basis for each
  - >-
    Chooses genuinely comparable benchmarks and breaks gaps into areas such as experience, cost, and
    reliability
  - States where the current solution sits, which gap is worth pursuing, and which is not
  - Identifies uncertain research samples or judgments and explains how to gather more evidence
