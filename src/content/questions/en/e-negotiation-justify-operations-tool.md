title: When you ask product and engineering for an operations tool, how do you justify it rather than keep asking engineers to pull data by hand?
question: When you ask the product and engineering teams for an operations tool, such as coupon configuration or automatic tagging, how do you justify its necessity to engineering rather than asking them every time to run a query by hand and pull the data?
intent: See how you win development resources with facts and the other side's point of view rather than only stating your own inconvenience.
signals:
  - Uses specific frequency, time spent, and error cases to show the real cost of the current way
  - Explains the business benefit and the engineering time saved, not only operations convenience
  - Proposes a clearly scoped request that can be staged and says its priority
  - Says how dependence on engineering is reduced before the tool exists
