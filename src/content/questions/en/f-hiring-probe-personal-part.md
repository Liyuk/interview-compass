title: A candidate keeps saying "we did...". How do you find out what they personally did?
question: A candidate keeps saying "we did..." when describing a project. How do you draw out the part they personally carried, especially how far they took part in the technology choices?
intent: See whether, as an interviewer, you can tell personal contribution from team results without making the candidate feel interrogated.
signals:
  - Asks about proposing, evaluating, building, and deciding separately, rather than one "what exactly did you do"
  - Verifies with specifics, such as the reason behind one particular decision and the alternatives
  - Leaves room for a frank answer about what they were not involved in, and does not penalize it
  - Compares the level of involvement observed with the scope of responsibility the candidate held
