title: How should lifecycle cost compare building an internal platform with buying one?
question: >-
  The team can build an internal identity service or purchase a vendor product; building better fits unusual
  workflows, while buying is faster but involves migration, compliance, and long-term licensing. How would you
  compare lifecycle cost and reversibility?
intent: Assess direct cost, dependency risk, and strategic control in build-versus-buy decisions.
signals:
  - "Calculates lifecycle cost across build or license, integration, migration, operations, support, compliance, and exit."
  - "Tests whether unusual workflows create durable differentiation that justifies internal ownership."
  - "Evaluates vendor dependency, data portability, service continuity, and realistic exit effort."
  - "Runs a bounded pilot with decision gates tied to total cost, fit, and a credible fallback."
