title: How should lifecycle cost compare building an internal platform with buying one?
question: >-
  The team can build an internal identity service or purchase a vendor product; building better fits unusual
  workflows, while buying is faster but involves migration, compliance, and long-term licensing. How would you
  compare lifecycle cost and reversibility?
intent: Assess direct cost, dependency risk, and strategic control in build-versus-buy decisions.
signals:
  - Includes development, operation, migration, support, compliance, and exit cost.
  - Evaluates vendor lock-in, data portability, service dependency, and differentiated needs.
  - Designs a pilot or exit criteria instead of treating the initial quote as total cost.
  - >-
    Define the target users, outcome to change, baseline, and guardrails for “Build-versus-buy boundary for a
    platform.”
  - >-
    Compare alternatives by value, causal evidence, resource needs, and opportunity cost, including what to
    defer.
  - Set signals for continuing, adjusting, expanding, or stopping, and explain how to verify the result.
