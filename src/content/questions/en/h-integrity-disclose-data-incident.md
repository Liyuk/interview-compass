title: How do you disclose a possible customer-data exposure before scope is known?
question: >-
  An access setting may have exposed some customers’ data to others, but scope is unconfirmed. Your
  manager worries early notice will cause alarm. How would you handle escalation and customer
  communication?
intent: >-
  Assess whether you escalate potential harm honestly, follow response procedures, and avoid
  delaying safeguards for reputation.
signals:
  - Preserves evidence, limits further exposure, and starts the incident-response process.
  - Separates confirmed facts from unknown scope and names notification owners and timing.
  - >-
    Updates affected populations and risk statements as the investigation proceeds, retaining
    revisions.
