title: Engineering says the full feature slips two weeks and the business wants it tomorrow. How do you quickly cut half and keep a version that can ship?
question: Engineering tells you that, because of the underlying architecture, the feature you specified will slip two weeks, while the business side wants it tomorrow. How do you cut half the features in a short time and keep a minimum viable version that can ship, and what do you rely on to decide what stays and what goes?
intent: See how you trade off around the core goal under time pressure rather than cutting evenly or delaying.
signals:
  - >-
    First verify that a safe, feasible minimum version exists; if constraints prevent it, explain which
    goals, dates, or commitments need renegotiation.
  - First confirms the core goal the business really wants to verify or solve
  - Decides what stays by the goal rather than personal preference and says the cost of what is cut
  - >-
    Confirms technical feasibility of the minimal version with engineering and agrees when the rest will
    be filled in
  - Tells the business plainly what this version can and cannot do, managing expectations
