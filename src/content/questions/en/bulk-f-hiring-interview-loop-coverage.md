title: Choose acceptable evidence gaps under a hiring deadline
question: >-
  The team must fill an on-call lead role within a month. A candidate is technically strong but has
  no incident-command experience. How would you decide which capabilities can be developed on the
  job and which evidence is essential before appointment?
intent: >-
  Examines whether hiring trade-offs center on essential responsibilities and risk rather than
  lowering standards or waiting indefinitely.
signals:
  - Separates non-delegable responsibilities from responsibilities that can be supported.
  - Assesses whether safeguards, pairing, and training can cover the experience gap.
  - Describes responsibilities, support, and early review candidly to the candidate.
