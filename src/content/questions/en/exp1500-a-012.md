title: How can an interviewer uncover real difficulty behind feature requests?
question: >-
  During user interviews, people repeatedly suggest buttons, filters, and reminders but rarely describe their
  goals or blocked situations. How would you adjust question order and wording to distinguish real
  experiences, stated preferences, and proposed solutions?
intent: >-
  Assess whether research questions elicit reliable facts instead of leading users toward a preferred
  solution.
signals:
  - Starts with a recent concrete behavior, goal, and context.
  - Avoids leading, hypothetical wording or embedding a proposed solution in the question.
  - Probes alternatives, impact, and current workarounds, separating facts from interpretation.
  - Ask about the most recent concrete task and obstacle.
  - Separate observed behavior, preferences, and proposed solutions.
  - Seek counterexamples and evidence from other contexts.
