title: How do you restore command when several owners issue conflicting incident instructions?
question: >-
  During an outage, engineering wants to disable a feature, business wants to keep accepting orders, and support
  has promised a recovery time. Responders receive conflicting directions. How would you organize the response?
intent: Assess whether you clarify incident command, source of truth, risk acceptance, and communication cadence quickly.
signals:
  - Assign one incident commander and a technical execution lead.
  - Separate recovery actions, business risk acceptance, and customer communications.
  - Maintain one status record, decision log, and next update time.
