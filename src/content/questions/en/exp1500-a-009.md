title: How would you isolate a slow dependency without exhausting the entry service?
question: An entry service calls several downstream systems. One noncritical dependency occasionally slows down, exhausting request threads and affecting unrelated features. How would you design timeouts, concurrency isolation, degradation, and recovery?
intent: Assess fault isolation, resource budgets, and recovery design after degradation.
signals:
  - Allocates timeouts from an end-to-end latency budget instead of adding arbitrary waits at each layer.
  - Explains the distinct problems addressed by pools, bulkheads, rate limits, circuit breakers, and fallbacks.
  - Defines acceptable degradation boundaries, recovery conditions, and user visibility.
