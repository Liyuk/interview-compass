title: How do you sequence work when delay is costly but evidence is incomplete?
question: >-
  A market window is short and a full solution takes two months. A reversible one-week pilot is
  possible, but cannot validate every long-term risk. How would you choose the sequence?
intent: >-
  Assess whether you combine opportunity window, information value, reversibility, and failure cost
  in staged decisions.
signals:
  - Estimates the business cost of delay and uncertainty a pilot could reduce.
  - Separates reversible investment, irreversible commitment, and risks the pilot cannot cover.
  - Defines when to pass, expand, adjust, or stop the pilot and by when.
