title: How do you measure engineering effectiveness, and how do you actually improve it after measuring?
question: How do you measure engineering effectiveness? Which metrics do you choose, how do you keep the numbers from being gamed, and how do you turn the measurement into real improvement?
intent: See whether you can use metrics to find real bottlenecks and avoid the distortions that measurement itself brings.
signals:
  - Chooses metrics that reflect the delivery process, such as lead time, change failure rate, and recovery time, and defines each
  - Says how a metric would be distorted and what companion metrics or practices guard against it
  - Uses the metrics to locate a specific bottleneck and then gives a specific improvement
  - Explains why metrics are not used for individual evaluation, or the risk of doing so
