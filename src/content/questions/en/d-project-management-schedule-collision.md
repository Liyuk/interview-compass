question: Two projects' schedules collide and both need the same people. What do you do, how do you turn the conflict into options that can be discussed, and how do you get both sides to accept the result?
intent: See how you handle a schedule conflict by turning it into a trade-off of goals and costs rather than a contest of who is more forceful.
signals:
  - First confirms each project's goal, the reason for its deadline, and the real staffing need
  - Offers options such as changing scope, order, people, or dates, with the cost of each
  - Says who has the authority to decide and when to escalate
  - After deciding, explains the reasons to the side not prioritized and agrees the condition for re-evaluation
