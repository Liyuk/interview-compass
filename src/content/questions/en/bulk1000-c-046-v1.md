title: How capability building enters a roadmap
question: >-
  The team needs months to build shared data capability, but the near-term roadmap shows no direct feature
  output. How would you express value, milestones, and exit criteria?
intent: >-
  Assess how you handle “How capability building enters a roadmap”: clarify goals and constraints, make an
  evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - >-
    Identify users and current problems served by shared data capability, including waiting, quality, and
    maintenance costs.
  - >-
    Compare shared investment, local improvements, procurement, and deferral by cost, adoption risk, and
    ongoing ownership.
  - >-
    Stage validation, first adoption, migration, and sustained use into milestones that can change
    subsequent investment.
  - >-
    Agree resources and exit criteria with authorized owners and verify use and outcomes rather than
    substituting platform delivery for benefits.
