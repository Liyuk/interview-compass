title: A product requirement as written is costly to build and will give a poor user experience. How do you bring an alternative and persuade them?
question: You receive a product requirement and find that building it as written is both costly technically and will give a poor user experience after launch. How do you bring a technical alternative and persuade product to change the requirement? Tell me about a real case.
intent: See how you negotiate with a proposal rather than just a refusal and stay cooperative when the other side judges differently.
signals:
  - First works out the goal product wants and then points out the cost and risk of the original requirement
  - Offers alternatives that reach the same goal and says the cost of each
  - Persuades with data or a prototype, not only technical authority
  - If the alternative is not adopted, says how it is carried out and how their own view is kept on record
