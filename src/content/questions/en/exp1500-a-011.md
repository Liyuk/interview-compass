title: How would you contain cascading costs from AI tool calls?
question: >-
  An AI assistant repeatedly calls search, databases, and external APIs for a small share of requests, making
  latency and cost far higher than average. How would you design per-task budgets, stop conditions,
  idempotency, and auditable execution records?
intent: Assess resource governance, tool-execution safety, and failure recovery in model-driven workflows.
signals:
  - Sets enforceable budgets for steps, time, tokens, and external calls separately.
  - Distinguishes read-only, repeatable, and side-effecting tools, with confirmation or idempotency controls.
  - Records each step's input, permission, outcome, and stop reason while limiting sensitive-data exposure.
  - Set per-task limits for duration, tool calls, steps, and cost.
  - Distinguish recoverable failures from repeated calls without progress, and define stopping conditions.
  - Identify redundant retrieval or steps and verify that optimization preserves task success.
  - Explain partial results, next steps, and audit records when a budget is exhausted.
