title: How do you turn an implementation disagreement into an agreement the team can deliver?

  Describe a time engineering found a technical constraint, undefined edge state, or unexpected cost
  while implementing your design. How did you clarify the real constraint, negotiate behavior and
  scope, and confirm that the final implementation still supported the core user task?

  Assesses whether the designer can solve implementation constraints with engineering, translate
  visual intent into clear behavioral agreements, and remain accountable for the shipped experience.

    Separates hard constraints, implementation cost, and missed communication instead of defending a
    mockup unilaterally
  - Uses a prototype, state inventory, or acceptance criteria to clarify acceptable alternatives
  - Checks critical paths after implementation and makes or records decisions about deviations
