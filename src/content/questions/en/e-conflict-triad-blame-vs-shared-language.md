title: When product, engineering, and operations blame one another, how do you get them to speak one shared business language?
question: Product blames engineering for being slow, engineering blames product for changing requirements daily, and operations blames product for missing tools and engineering for instability. How do you turn this mutual blame into everyone speaking one shared business language and taking joint responsibility for results?
intent: See how you build common goals and language in a cross-functional team so each function's value is visible to the others.
signals:
  - Restates each side's accusations as concrete facts and constraints rather than judging who is right
  - Builds common goals and measures so each function's costs and benefits can be accounted for by the others
  - Helps each function understand the others' constraints, such as product understanding technical cost and engineering understanding business goals
  - Holds this cooperation in place through joint reviews and decision mechanisms rather than one conversation
