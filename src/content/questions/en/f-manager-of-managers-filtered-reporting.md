title: You find one manager is hiding that a core system is at high risk while another reports problems daily. How do you break the filtering of information?
question: You oversee several first-line managers. You find manager A, to protect the department's performance, is hiding that their core system is at high risk, while manager B reports problems daily. How do you break this filtering of information so bad news can be said early and safely?
intent: See whether you realize your own rewards and penalties shape how people report, and can use mechanisms and behavior to make honesty the rational choice.
signals:
  - Reflects on whether their own reactions make the honest lose out and the concealing gain
  - Treats the concealing behavior and the risk separately, not only by outcome
  - Builds channels of information independent of reporting, such as metrics, incident records, and spot checks
  - Openly rewards early surfacing of problems and keeps it from counting against performance
