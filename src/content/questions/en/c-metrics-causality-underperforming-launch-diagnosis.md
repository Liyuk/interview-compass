title: For the last feature you launched, how did the core metric change before and after, and where do you look when the numbers fall short?
question: For the last new feature you launched, what were the specific before-and-after changes in the core metric, such as conversion, click-through, or retention? If the numbers fall short of expectation, along which dimensions do you usually investigate the cause?
intent: See whether you know the numbers you own and have a systematic method for investigating results below expectation.
signals:
  - Gives specific before-and-after figures with the definition and comparison period, not a vague "it improved a lot"
  - Investigates layer by layer through funnel, user path, segments, and time, and says what each rules out
  - Separates problems in the feature itself, in reach, in metric definition, and in outside factors
  - Says what decision followed the investigation and how it was verified
