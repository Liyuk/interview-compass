title: You attribute a business result to your project. How solid is that causal chain?
question: You attribute a business result to your project. How solid is that causal chain? What evidence supports each link, which link is weakest, and are there other explanations?
intent: See whether you can break a causal argument into links and honestly rate the strength of evidence for each, rather than telling one smooth story.
signals:
  - Breaks the causal chain into specific links and gives evidence for each
  - Names the weakest link and how it could be strengthened
  - Raises other possible explanations on their own and says how they were ruled out or admitted as uncertain
  - Separates correlation from causation, and conclusions from experiments from those from observation
