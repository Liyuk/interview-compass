signals: ["State the project goal, time horizon, decision authority, and non-negotiable constraints before explaining criteria and priority.", "Use the same criteria to explain expected impact, evidence, and resource cost for three priorities and the loss from two deferrals or stops.", "Separate facts, assumptions, and external changes; identify dependencies, risks, and whose agreement the trade-off requires.", "Define progress or disconfirming signals, review timing, and thresholds to stop or resume investment."]
weakAnswer: "Listing five items by personal interest or urgency without shared criteria, resource cost, impact of deferred work, or evidence that would change the decision."
anchors:
  notPassing: "Provides a list without goal, criteria, or opportunity cost."

  excellent: "Applies criteria consistently across outcomes, evidence, dependencies, and resources and gives reviewable reversal signals."
  exceptional: "Connects ranking to actual capacity and authority, explains counterfactual cost and review cadence, and uses outcomes to recalibrate the criteria."
