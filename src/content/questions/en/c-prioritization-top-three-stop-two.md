  Understand whether you set the ranking criterion before owning the trade-off, and can explain why the
  shelved items existed in the first place.

  - States the goal, time range, and standard behind the ranking before giving the answer
  - Explains for each item why it matters or why it can stop
  - >-
    Distinguishes a changed environment from a wrong earlier judgment, without blaming the past with today's
    results
