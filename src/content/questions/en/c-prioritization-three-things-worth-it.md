title: Looking back over the past six months, what were the three most worthwhile things you did?
question: Looking back over the past six months, what were the three most worthwhile things you did? Why those, what benefit did they bring, and how did you confirm that benefit?
intent: See what your yardstick for "worthwhile" is, and whether you can show a thing's benefit with evidence that can be checked.
signals:
  - States the yardstick for "worthwhile" and the time range before naming the three
  - Each benefit has a way of being verified, not only a description
  - Separates what came from your own judgment from what the environment provided
