title: Your team had a poor year, bonuses shrank and promotion slots were cut, and two key members start looking outside. What do you do?
question: Your team received a poor performance rating this year, year-end bonuses shrank across the board, and promotion slots were cut. Two key members on the team are starting to look for opportunities outside. How do you talk with them and manage expectations, and what non-material ways do you use to keep them?
intent: See how, when you cannot offer material rewards, you communicate honestly and find ways that truly retain people rather than making empty promises.
signals:
  - Explains the situation and reasons honestly without exaggerating or deflecting, and learns what each of the two cares about
  - Promises only what can be delivered and says plainly what cannot
  - Offers non-material things, such as greater responsibility, growth opportunities, and real recognition of their contribution
  - Accepts that retention may fail and reduces the team's dependence on the two in advance
