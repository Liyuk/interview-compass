title: How do you preserve historical reports when categories change?
question: >-
  Business categories may be added, merged, or renamed. Reports must reproduce the definition used at the time
  and compare results under the current definition. How would you preserve source categories, mapping
  versions, and report definitions?
intent: >-
  Assess whether you can preserve historical facts as classifications change and clearly distinguish what
  people saw then from results restated under a new definition.
signals:
  - Retains the category recorded at the time instead of storing only the current mapping.
  - Versions category definitions and mappings with effective dates.
  - >-
    Lets readers choose the historical or current definition and explains cases that cannot be mapped
    unambiguously.
  - Prevents trend comparisons from treating restated results as the reports originally shown.
  - Separate stable IDs, historical names, and current names.
  - Specify time semantics for different reports.
  - Prevent a rename from rewriting financial records.
