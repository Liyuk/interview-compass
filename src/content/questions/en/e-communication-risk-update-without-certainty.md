title: How do you update leadership before a risk is understood?
question: >-
  A vendor risk may affect a launch, but neither cause nor likelihood is clear. Leadership wants an answer
  today. How would you report facts, unknowns, safeguards, and the next update time?
intent: "Assess whether you communicate risk accurately when facts and probability are incomplete, enabling decision-makers to see what is known, unknown, protected, and due for the next update."
signals: ["Separate verified facts, assumptions, unknowns, and sources without turning guesses into probability claims.", "State affected users, systems, scope, and worst-case impact alongside current mitigation.", "Identify which decisions need evidence gates and which allow reversible protection now.", "Name validation owners, the next information point and update time, and how evidence changes will be shared."]
anchors:
  notPassing: "Describes a vague risk without separating fact from unknown."
  passing: "States impact and one protection step with a clear next update."
  excellent: "Connects fact/assumption, impact, mitigation, validation owner, and decision evidence."
  exceptional: "Gives only supportable probability ranges, distinguishes reversible from irreversible action, and maintains a communication record that changes with evidence."
