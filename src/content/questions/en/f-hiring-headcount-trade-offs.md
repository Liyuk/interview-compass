question: Five teams each ask for another senior lead, and the budget covers two. How do you decide which two to hire first, and which problems you will not solve by hiring? How do you explain the decision to the teams that did not get a slot?
intent: See whether you treat a hiring plan as a set of trade-offs rather than the sum of each team's request, and can say what was given up.
signals:
  - Starts from the organizational problem or risk each role would address, not from who asks loudest or which team is largest
  - Separates gaps that call for hiring from those better met by development, reassignment, temporary delegation, or a smaller goal
  - States the basis for the ranking, when deferred roles will be revisited, and what is explicitly given up
  - Tells the teams left out the basis and the review date, without disclosing personnel details that should stay private
