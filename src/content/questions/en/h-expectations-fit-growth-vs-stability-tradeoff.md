title: How do you discuss expectations when a candidate wants growth but the role is stable?
question: >-
  A candidate wants new domains and broader scope within a year, but the role mainly operates a mature system
  reliably. How would you assess fit and describe realistic growth paths?
intent: Assess whether you explain the role, opportunities, and uncertainty honestly so both sides can choose knowingly.
signals:
  - Describe current work mix, rate of change, and responsibility scope.
  - Ask how the candidate has grown through stable work in the past.
  - Separate promised development from opportunities dependent on organizational change.
