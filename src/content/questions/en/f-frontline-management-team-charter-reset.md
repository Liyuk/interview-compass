title: "After a team grows, how do you clarify goals and responsibilities?"
question: >-
  After growing from six to fourteen people, the team still uses its old division of work. Members disagree on
  architecture decisions, operations support, and quality ownership. How would you reset the team agreement?
intent: Assess whether you recalibrate goals, responsibilities, decision rights, and collaboration rules as a team grows.
signals:
  - Identify actual examples of changed goals, ownership gaps, and duplicate work.
  - Define outcome ownership, decision rights, and interfaces with the team.
  - Review frequently to see whether the agreement improves delivery and conflict.
