title: How do you judge and shape a team's atmosphere?
question: How do you judge and shape a team's atmosphere? When it is poor, what signs do you see first, and how do you make people willing to surface problems and disagreement?
intent: See whether you can turn team atmosphere into specific behavior that can be observed and changed, rather than stopping at a slogan such as "be open".
signals:
  - Observe climate through timely bad news, how dissent is heard, and fair access to opportunities.
  - Explain management responses to mistakes, disagreement, and uncertainty, distinguishing support from ignoring problems.
  - Review facts and system conditions before assigning blame, avoid humiliation or prejudgment, and handle behavioral responsibility using evidence and actual authority.
  - Verify mechanisms through independent feedback, work records, and subsequent changes rather than impressions or surveys alone.
