title: A task from your mentor leaves you with no progress after a day. Do you keep digging alone or ask for help, and where is your line?
question: A task from your mentor leaves you with no progress after a full day and you are completely stuck. Do you keep researching alone or go to your mentor, and where do you draw the line between thinking independently and wasting time?
intent: See whether you have a standard for when to ask for help, neither leaning on others too early nor staying stuck out of pride and delaying delivery.
signals:
  - Gives concrete criteria, such as a time limit, directions already tried, and how urgent the task is
  - Organizes the problem before asking, saying what has been tried and where it is stuck
  - Says how risk is surfaced early so others have time to adjust
  - Follows up on what was learned after asking, avoiding asking again about the same kind of problem
