title: >-
  How do you set acceptable trade-offs when an accessibility requirement conflicts with a launch
  date?
question: >-
  A critical flow is two weeks from launch when a review finds that keyboard users cannot complete
  one step. A full redesign would affect the date. How would you assess the impact, negotiate scope
  with the team, and explain what can be staged versus what cannot safely ship?
intent: >-
  Assesses whether the candidate treats accessibility as a real user impact and delivery risk, and
  can set boundaries and staged plans under pressure.
signals:
  - >-
    Identifies affected users, tasks, and the degree of blockage instead of reducing the issue to
    visual preference
  - >-
    Proposes short-term mitigation that reduces the actual barrier and a follow-up fix with an owner
    and date
  - >-
    States the launch threshold, who accepts any remaining risk, and when to escalate if the
    threshold cannot be met
