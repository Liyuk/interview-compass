title: How should shared capacity be allocated when every project is top priority?
question: >-
  Every initiative in the quarterly portfolio is labeled top priority, but Product, Data, and Security lack
  capacity to support them all. How would you standardize priority definitions, expose dependencies, and make
  resource trade-offs?
intent: Assess portfolio-level capacity allocation and priority calibration.
signals:
  - >-
    Distinguish mandatory obligations, strategic outcomes, and adjustable opportunities, checking the
    basis and timing of each top-priority claim.
  - >-
    Compare the portfolio by critical paths, shared capacity, delay loss, and opportunity cost, exposing
    conflicting goals.
  - >-
    Have authorized decision owners confirm stopping, deferring, narrowing, or adding capacity and make
    forgone outcomes explicit.
  - Update dependencies, resources, and commitments together, stating what new evidence triggers review.
