title: How should shared capacity be allocated when every project is top priority?
question: >-
  Every initiative in the quarterly portfolio is labeled top priority, but Product, Data, and Security lack
  capacity to support them all. How would you standardize priority definitions, expose dependencies, and make
  resource trade-offs?
intent: Assess portfolio-level capacity allocation and priority calibration.
signals:
  - Distinguishes legal or risk obligations, strategic goals, and adjustable opportunities.
  - Evaluates the portfolio by dependency chains, critical paths, shared capacity, and delay cost.
  - Makes explicit stop, defer, or add-capacity decisions and adjusts goals accordingly.
  - >-
    Define the target users, outcome to change, baseline, and guardrails for “Ordering several “top
    priorities”.”
  - >-
    Compare alternatives by value, causal evidence, resource needs, and opportunity cost, including what to
    defer.
  - Set signals for continuing, adjusting, expanding, or stopping, and explain how to verify the result.
