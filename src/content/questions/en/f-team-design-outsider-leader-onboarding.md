title: As a leader brought in from outside, how do you fit into the existing team?
question: As a leader brought in from outside, how do you fit into the existing team? How do you get to know the people and the work, build your own sources of information, and avoid overturning existing practice from day one?
intent: See how you quickly build a basis for judgment in an unfamiliar setting while winning the team's trust.
signals:
  - Listens before changing, learning people and work through one-on-ones and real work rather than only reports
  - Builds several independent sources of information and cross-checks them to avoid being swayed by a single channel
  - Separates what should change soon from what should be observed first, with the basis
  - States their own role, expectations, and way of working while respecting existing contributions
