title: Use one portfolio project to show how your design contribution can be verified.
question: >-
  Choose a real project from your portfolio and walk through it from the initial problem to the
  post-launch outcome. What design work did you personally do, which key trade-offs are visible in
  artifacts such as documents or prototypes, and what evidence can verify the result?
intent: >-
  Assesses whether the candidate can turn a portfolio presentation into traceable work evidence and
  distinguish individual contribution, team output, and outcome attribution.
signals:
  - >-
    Supports the story with concrete prototypes, flows, or decision records rather than only
    polished screens
  - >-
    Explains personal ownership and the division of work with product, research, and engineering
    partners
  - Separates verified outcomes, reasonable inferences, and changes that cannot be attributed
