title: How do you explain technical risk and choices to an audience unfamiliar with the system?
question: >-
  A storage migration could briefly hide some customer data, but the business owner does not know replication lag
  or rollback limits. How would you explain risk and enable a sound decision?
intent: >-
  Assess whether you translate technical mechanisms into customer impact, likelihood, reversibility, and business
  options.
signals:
  - Start with affected people, time window, and visible outcome.
  - Explain likelihood, mitigations, and unknowns in plain language.
  - Offer proceed, stage, or delay options with their costs.
