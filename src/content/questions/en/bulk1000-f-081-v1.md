title: Promotion evidence mismatches role scope
question: >-
  A candidate lists many project outcomes but cannot show sustained ownership at the target level. How would
  you distinguish results, impact scope, and responsibility evidence?
intent: >-
  Assess how you handle “Promotion evidence mismatches role scope”: clarify goals and constraints, make an
  evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - >-
    Compare target-stage responsibilities with sustained judgments, actions, and outcomes rather than
    substituting project count for responsibility evidence.
  - >-
    Separate personal contribution, team outcomes, external conditions, and acting assignments; state
    limits to causal attribution.
  - >-
    Identify work records and observation periods that can fill gaps, distinguishing missing opportunity,
    insufficient evidence, and unmet expectations.
