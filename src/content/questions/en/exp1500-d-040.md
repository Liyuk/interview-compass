title: How can downstream systems be protected when a message backlog resumes?
question: >-
  A consumer failure leaves a queue hours behind; after repair, many old messages will replay into billing,
  notifications, and analytics, which have different capacities and side effects. How would you control
  recovery rate and protect consumers?
intent: Assess queue management, downstream capacity, and side-effect control during recovery.
signals:
  - Estimates backlog size, message age, priority, and downstream throughput limits.
  - Separates discardable, deferrable, required, and side-effecting messages.
  - Restores processing in stages while monitoring errors, duplicates, and downstream lag.
  - Set separate rate and concurrency budgets for replay.
  - Adapt using downstream latency, errors, and live traffic.
  - Prevent live messages from starvation behind backlog.
