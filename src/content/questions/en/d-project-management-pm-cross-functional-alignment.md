title: "How do you give engineering, design, and operations shared goals and clear ownership?"
question: "You are leading a project across engineering, design, and operations. How would you clarify the shared goal and responsibilities when each group defines success differently?"
intent: From a product manager's view, see how you align several parties on a shared goal and clear interfaces without direct authority over them.
signals:
  - Puts each party's goals, constraints, and success criteria on the table and finds the goal that is truly shared
  - Fixes deliverables, interfaces, acceptance criteria, time windows, and owners
  - Agrees how information is shared and when risks are escalated
  - When success criteria conflict, decides by the shared goal and facts, not by rank
