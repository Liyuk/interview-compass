title: How do you decide what to do when user research findings conflict?
question: >-
  In research for a new feature, a small group of frequent users strongly supports the concept,
  while novice participants repeatedly fail the core task and the analytics show no clear
  difference. How would you interpret what each piece of evidence can tell you and decide whether to
  proceed, revise, or run more research?
intent: >-
  Assesses whether the candidate can recognize the limits of incomplete, conflicting research and
  choose a next step proportionate to the decision risk.
signals:
  - >-
    Clarifies the research population, task context, and sample before treating stated preference as
    behavioral evidence
  - Names what remains uncertain and how that uncertainty affects the decision
  - >-
    Proposes targeted validation that distinguishes competing explanations instead of adding
    interviews without a purpose
