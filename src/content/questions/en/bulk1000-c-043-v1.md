title: Experiments competing with maintenance work
question: >-
  The team needs to test a growth hypothesis while customer-impacting maintenance work keeps arriving. How
  would you set capacity, triggers, and stop rules?
intent: >-
  Assess how you handle “Experiments competing with maintenance work”: clarify goals and constraints, make an
  evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - Verify maintenance impact, risk, and historical load alongside growth hypotheses and timing.
  - >-
    Compare protected maintenance, reduced scope, reserves, and staged experiments by value and
    opportunity cost.
  - >-
    State which safety or service commitments cannot be displaced and who decides when capacity is
    exceeded.
  - >-
    Review maintenance risk, user outcomes, and learning together when continuing, pausing, or
    reordering.
