intent: See how you keep a team's sense of direction and trust in an unfavorable environment without sugar-coating reality.
signals:
  - Tells the team plainly about the situation and the uncertainty rather than glossing over or avoiding it
  - Narrows the goal to what the team can control and says why it is worth doing
  - Pays attention to each person's situation and development, not only overall delivery
  - Says how changes in morale are noticed and how key people are kept
