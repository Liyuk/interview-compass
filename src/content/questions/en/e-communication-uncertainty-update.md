title: How do you communicate uncertainty when a decision window has arrived?
question: >-
  A launch decision must be made today, but one key experiment result is missing. How would you
  distinguish facts, assumptions, unknown risks, and reversible options in your update?
intent: >-
  Assess whether you turn uncertainty into decision-ready information instead of hiding gaps or
  delaying with vague language.
signals:
  - Separates observed facts, inferences, untested assumptions, and possible impact.
  - Presents options with risk owners, rollback paths, and plans to gather missing evidence.
  - States what new evidence would change the recommendation and sets a review time.
