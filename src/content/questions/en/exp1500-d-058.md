title: How would you diagnose an operations pilot that degrades at scale?
question: >-
  A human-operated pilot worked in two regions, but after expansion to ten, handling time and error rates both
  rise. How would you identify scaling bottlenecks and adjust the operating model?
intent: Assess understanding of pilot conditions, scale effects, and process-capacity constraints.
signals:
  - Compare staffing, inputs, demand, exceptions, and support between pilot and expansion regions.
  - >-
    Locate training, tooling, staffing, rule, and span bottlenecks without attributing scale problems
    directly to individuals.
  - >-
    Compare standardization, automation, local adaptation, and slower rollout by cost and unintended
    harm.
  - Validate throughput, errors, waiting, and sustained load in stages, with expansion or stop criteria.
