title: How would you diagnose an operations pilot that degrades at scale?
question: >-
  A human-operated pilot worked in two regions, but after expansion to ten, handling time and error rates both
  rise. How would you identify scaling bottlenecks and adjust the operating model?
intent: Assess understanding of pilot conditions, scale effects, and process-capacity constraints.
signals:
  - Compares staffing, input quality, volume, and exception rates between pilot and scaled regions.
  - Identifies whether bottlenecks come from training, tools, scheduling, rules, or management span.
  - Uses staged adjustments to establish throughput and quality limits.
  - >-
    Turn “A successful pilot fails to scale” into an acceptable outcome, work items, owners, and
    prerequisites.
  - >-
    Explain how capacity, critical paths, risks, and conflicting commitments become visible and trigger
    replanning or escalation.
  - Define milestones, completion evidence, acceptance gates, and conditions for handoff to ongoing owners.
