title: Why did you leave your last job, or why are you considering leaving your current one?
question: Why did you leave your last job, or why are you considering leaving your current one? Did you try to resolve it internally, and what did you yourself weigh and give up in the choice?
intent: See how you honestly explain a departure without putting all the responsibility on others, and whether what you want fits what this job can offer.
signals:
  - Gives the real reason rather than a rehearsed line
  - Says what was tried before leaving and what part of it was their own responsibility
  - Stays fair to the former employer and colleagues and does not explain by disparaging
  - Says what they are looking for and how they would tell whether the new place provides it
