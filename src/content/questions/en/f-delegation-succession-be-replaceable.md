title: If you are to take on something broader and someone else will take over your position, how do you make that happen smoothly?
question: If you are to take on something broader and someone else will take over your position, how do you prepare in advance so that your work can be picked up rather than going wrong after you leave?
intent: See whether you treat being replaceable as a manager's responsibility and develop a successor and capture mechanisms ahead of time.
signals:
  - Says how early and in what way the successor is exposed to real decisions, rather than a handover only before leaving
  - Separates knowledge that can be written down from judgment that lives only in your head and tries to capture the latter
  - Says how the team and stakeholders are brought to accept the new owner
  - Says how it is checked after leaving that the handover really worked
