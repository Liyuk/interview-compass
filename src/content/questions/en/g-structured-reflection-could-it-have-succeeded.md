title: Under what conditions could that failed project have succeeded?
question: Under what conditions could that failed project have succeeded? Could you have had those conditions at the time, and how would you design it now for a better chance?
intent: See whether you can draw concrete conditions for success out of a failure, rather than stopping at "try harder next time", and judge whether they were feasible.
signals:
  - Gives concrete conditions for success, such as goal, resources, scope, or timing, not a general attitude
  - Judges whether those conditions were attainable then, and on what basis
  - Says how it would be designed now and how key assumptions would be verified earlier
  - Admits that some projects might not have succeeded even if redone
