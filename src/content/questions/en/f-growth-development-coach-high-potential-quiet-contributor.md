title: How do you give fair growth evidence to a high-potential but low-visibility contributor?
question: >-
  An engineer quietly reduces failures in backend systems, while another frequently presents cross-team work and is
  better known to leadership. You calibrate development opportunities. How do you prevent visibility from replacing
  impact?
intent: Assess whether you recognize visibility bias and create opportunities and evidence tied to role capability.
signals:
  - Collect measurable impact such as reliability, effort saved, and avoided risk.
  - Ensure evaluation covers actual scope rather than only public performance.
  - Offer assignments that broaden influence and equalize feedback access.
