title: A competitor offers a requested feature for free, but you see no churn. Would you follow?
question: >-
  A major competitor makes a feature free that large customers have repeatedly requested, but you have not
  observed customer losses because of it. How would you determine whether this is an emerging competitive
  risk, a negotiation signal, or a feature gap not worth following?
intent: >-
  Assess whether the candidate distinguishes customer voice, competitive intelligence, and behavioral evidence
  while making a decision in the context of their own positioning.
signals:
  - Separates customer statements, actual switching behavior, and the competitor feature’s target audience
  - Identifies limits in how current data reveals churn, renewal, or procurement decisions
  - Proposes a next step that tests the risk without presuming the competitor should be copied
  - Separates competitive necessity, genuine user demand, and marketing attention.
  - >-
    Evaluates implementation and ongoing validation cost, error responsibility, and product-positioning
    impact.
  - Defines test or rejection criteria instead of using a competitor launch as the roadmap rationale.
