title: The organization decides to split your team and hand some people and work to other teams. How do you handle it?
question: The organization decides to split your team and hand some people and work to other teams. How do you handle the people issues and the work issues, and how do you keep the handover from going wrong?
intent: See how you look after both people and work during an organizational change and make the handover reliable and the people fairly treated.
signals:
  - First understands the reason and scope of the change before deciding how to explain it to the team
  - For people, says how to communicate, hear their wishes, and treat who goes and who stays fairly
  - For work, says how responsibilities, dependencies, and leftover issues are listed and handed over in writing
  - Says how collaboration with both sides continues afterwards and how they see their own changed role
