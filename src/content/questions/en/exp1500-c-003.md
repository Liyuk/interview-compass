title: What if first-day completion rises while long-term retention falls?
question: >-
  A mobile onboarding redesign helps more users complete setup on day one, but active retention drops two
  weeks later; the flow also requests more permissions. How would you judge whether short-term conversion
  masks lower long-term value or loss of trust?
intent: Assess product-value judgment across long-term behavior, trust, and user effort.
signals:
  - Analyzes cohorts across first task, ongoing use, and exit paths.
  - Validates the relationship among permission requests, time-to-value, and user expectations.
  - Defines short- and long-term measures, guardrails, and decision criteria for reversible experiments.
  - Segment cohorts by acquisition channel, intent, and task success.
  - Check completion definitions and retention windows.
  - Design experiments that distinguish user quality from experience mechanisms.
