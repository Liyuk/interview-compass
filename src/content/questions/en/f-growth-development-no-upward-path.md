question: Some people on the team cannot move up for now, perhaps because promotion slots are limited or they are one step short. How do you adjust the team and give them new work and direction without making them feel shelved?
intent: See how you keep creating growth and value for members when the path upward is limited, while staying honest.
signals:
  - Explains the situation and reasons honestly rather than avoiding it or promising what cannot be delivered
  - Gives concrete new work, such as wider reach, a new area, or mentoring, and says what it is worth
  - Separates lateral growth from vertical promotion and lets the person take part in choosing
  - Says how others on the team come to see the arrangement as fair
