title: Duplicated investment across a project portfolio
question: >-
  Three projects independently build similar permission and notification capabilities, each seeming reasonable
  alone. How would you detect portfolio-level duplication and change governance?
intent: >-
  Assess how you handle “Duplicated investment across a project portfolio”: clarify goals and constraints,
  make an evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - >-
    Verify whether permission and notification needs are actually shared, separating reuse from business
    differences.
  - >-
    Compare separate implementations, shared capabilities, and gradual integration by development,
    migration, maintenance, and coordination cost.
  - >-
    Agree ownership, capacity, and change rules with authorized owners without assuming centralization is
    better.
  - >-
    Validate governance through adoption, delivery waiting, and total cost rather than merged-project
    counts.
