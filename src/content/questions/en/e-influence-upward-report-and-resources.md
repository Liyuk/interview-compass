title: When you give an overall report upward, how do you present it and win more resources for the team?
question: When you give an overall report upward, how do you present it and win more resources for the team? When different organizations have different goals, how do you connect your team's goals to theirs?
intent: See whether you organize a report around the decision-maker's goals and argue for resources with evidence rather than a list of needs.
signals:
  - Works out the decision-maker's goals and constraints before deciding what to say
  - Connects the team's goals to those of different organizations and says what each side gains
  - Argues for resources with facts, expected results, and risks, and offers alternatives with a recommendation
  - When resources are not granted, says how the commitment is narrowed rather than pushing on regardless
