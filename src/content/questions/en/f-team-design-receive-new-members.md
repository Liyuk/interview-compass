title: The organization merges a group of new people and new work into your team. How do you make them truly fit in?
question: The organization merges a group of new people and new work into your team. How do you make them truly fit in, and how do you handle unease, division of work, and differing standards between existing and new members?
intent: See how you integrate two groups of people and work with different backgrounds and avoid a gap between "new" and "old" members.
signals:
  - First learns the new members' background, their work, and how they feel about the change instead of applying old rules at once
  - Makes responsibility boundaries, priorities, and shared standards clear for old and new work
  - Pays attention to existing members' unease, such as a diluted role, and communicates proactively
  - Says how it is checked that integration really happened, not only by meetings held
