intent: See how you treat long-standing problem behavior without labeling someone in advance and without letting it keep harming the team.
signals:
  - Judges from specific behavior and facts they observe themselves, not only from what a predecessor or others say
  - Tells the person clearly the specific behavior, its impact, and the expectation, with a chance and a deadline to improve
  - Protects the team meanwhile, for example by changing how people work together and recording facts, rather than waiting
  - If improvement fails, describes the steps taken under policy and keeps the process fair and compliant
