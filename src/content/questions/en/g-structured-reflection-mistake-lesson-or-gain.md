title: Tell me about something you got wrong. How do you see it?
question: "Tell me about something you got wrong. How do you see it: did it leave you an experience, a gain, or just a lesson? In what specific later situation did you actually act differently?"
intent: See how you treat your own mistakes, and whether "I learned something" shows up as observable behavior change rather than as part of packaging a project.
signals:
  - Explains what the judgment rested on at the time, not only that the outcome was bad
  - Separates experience, gain, and lesson, and says what each means
  - Points to a specific later situation where practice changed, rather than a vague "I grew"
  - Does not dress the mistake up as "actually a good thing", and does not over-blame themselves
