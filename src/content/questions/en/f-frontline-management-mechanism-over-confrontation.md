title: A technically strong developer on your team dislikes writing tests and reviews carelessly. How do you change that with mechanisms rather than only conversation?
question: A strong-willed, technically good developer on your team dislikes writing unit tests and reviews carelessly. Talking to them directly may annoy them. What rules or mechanisms would you use to change the behavior, and how do you make them open and fair to everyone?
intent: See how you shape engineering habits with transparent mechanisms rather than covert manipulation or relying only on personal persuasion.
signals:
  - Separates behavior that conversation can fix from behavior that needs a mechanism to hold
  - Gives concrete mechanisms, such as quality gates in the release pipeline, minimum review requirements, and visible measures
  - Makes the mechanisms open and consistent for everyone, not aimed at one person
  - Also talks with the person about reasons and expectations and follows up on whether behavior changed
