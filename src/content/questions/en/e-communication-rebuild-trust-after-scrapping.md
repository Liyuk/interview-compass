title: Because of insufficient up-front research you halted or overturned a plan halfway through engineering's work. How do you rebuild their trust?
question: Tell me about a real time, because of insufficient up-front research, you halted or substantially overturned a plan when engineering was halfway through. How did you treat it, and how did you reassure the engineering team and rebuild trust?
intent: See how you own the cost of your own insufficient judgment and let others see you really changed how you work.
signals:
  - Admits plainly that the problem lay in their own preparation rather than blaming a change in the business
  - Names the concrete cost to engineering, takes the finished work seriously, and keeps what can be reused
  - Says what specifically changed afterwards, such as validation before requirement review and agreements about changes
  - Rebuilds trust through later behavior rather than words
