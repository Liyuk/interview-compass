title: How do you negotiate scope around an immovable deadline?
question: >-
  A partner insists that an event date cannot move, while engineering estimates the full solution will be two weeks
  late. You need a deliverable version. How would you negotiate scope, quality, and operational readiness?
intent: Assess whether you separate fixed constraints from negotiable variables and reach executable commitments.
signals:
  - Confirm the real constraint behind the date and cost of moving it.
  - Split the proposal into core outcome, deferred capability, risk, and acceptance criteria.
  - Name who bears extra cost, the decision deadline, and a fallback if criteria are missed.
