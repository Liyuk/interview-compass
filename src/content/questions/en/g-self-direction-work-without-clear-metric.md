title: How do you decide whether to continue valuable work that lacks a reliable metric?
question: >-
  You own internal data quality with no shared defect baseline or formal business target, but errors affect several
  downstream teams. How would you define a goal and verify progress?
intent: Assess whether you establish evidence, temporary measures, and review points when goals are unclear.
signals:
  - Define affected workflows, error types, and current risk.
  - Create a lightweight baseline and representative samples without waiting for perfect data.
  - Set short-term improvement measures and a checkpoint to reconfirm the goal.
