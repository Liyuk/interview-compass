title: What were your actual contribution and output in this project, and how can others verify them?
question: What were your actual contribution and output in this project? Which records could others use to verify them, such as design documents, code changes, decision records, or metric dashboards?
intent: See whether the contribution you describe comes with evidence others can check, not only a spoken account.
signals:
  - Ties the contribution to concrete outputs rather than adjectives
  - Names which records let others verify it, and what they can and cannot prove
  - Separates your own output from the team's
