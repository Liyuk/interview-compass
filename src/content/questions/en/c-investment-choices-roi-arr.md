intent: See whether you can put inputs and benefits on one definition and are clear about the assumptions and limits of the numbers.
signals:
  - Says what the inputs consist of (people, infrastructure, opportunity cost) and how benefits are defined
  - Says what a metric such as ARR does and does not represent, for example churn and one-off revenue
  - Names the least certain assumption in the estimate and gives a range rather than a single number
  - Separates benefits attributed directly from those that arrive indirectly
