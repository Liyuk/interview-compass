title: When supply and demand badly diverge locally on a two-sided platform, how do you restore balance quickly with pricing or subsidies?
question: On a two-sided platform such as ride-hailing, food delivery, or content, supply and demand badly diverge in one area, for example orders surge in the rain with few drivers, or a newly opened city has supply but no users. How do you design dynamic pricing or targeted subsidies to restore balance quickly without overspending the budget or harming the long-term ecosystem?
intent: See your grasp of two-sided market mechanics and how you trade short-term balance against the long-term ecosystem.
signals:
  - First judges whether the imbalance is temporary or structural and which side is the bottleneck
  - Designs different levers for each side, such as incentives for the scarce side and guidance for the surplus side
  - States the cap, the trigger conditions, and the way out for prices or subsidies to avoid dependence
  - Uses data to monitor whether balance is restored and the side effects on the other side's experience
