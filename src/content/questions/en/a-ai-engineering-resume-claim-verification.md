question: A resume line saying "used AI to improve engineering efficiency" carries little information. How would you rewrite it into an experience others can probe and verify?
intent: See whether you can state how AI was used on one concrete task, where its limits were, and how the result was checked, rather than only naming a tool.
signals:
  - States the task, AI role, personal judgment, and verification artifacts beyond tool names.
  - Uses observed errors, reviews, and outcomes to bound AI use and separates facts from expected benefits.
  - Compares manual, assisted, and automated work by evidence and cost without a required assistance-only conclusion.

weakAnswer: Turns “used an AI tool” into an efficiency claim without separating personal judgment, AI output, and verified facts.
anchors:
  notPassing: Lists an AI tool or efficiency claim without a concrete task or verifiable result.
  passing: Describes the task, personal actions, where AI contributed, and one verifiable outcome.
  excellent: Shows how review caught errors and separates actual contribution from expected benefit.
  exceptional: Compares manual and AI-assisted quality/cost and explains when automation should not be used.
