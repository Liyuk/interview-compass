title: The job description promises room for responsibility the team cannot actually give, and interviews keep making candidates prove themselves. How do you keep the hiring process honest with candidates?
question: A job description promises room for responsibility the team cannot actually give, and the interview stages keep asking candidates questions unrelated to the role. How do you keep the hiring process honest with candidates without revealing information that should stay private?
intent: See whether you treat candidates as people who are also judging the organization, and keep the role's promise, the process, and the communication consistent.
signals:
  - Checks the description against the real work, authority, and resources, and fixes the description rather than relying on the interview to dress it up
  - Lets candidates know the real responsibilities, what is being assessed, the stages, and the expected timing, and cuts interview cost unrelated to the role
  - Keeps rejection communication truthful, respectful, and consistent, and decides on personalized feedback by organizational policy and circumstances
  - Says which personnel information is not shared, and avoids misleading promises while keeping it private
