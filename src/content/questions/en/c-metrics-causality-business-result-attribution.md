title: What business result did your project bring?
question: >-
  What business result did your project bring? How do you confirm it came from this project and not from
  something else?
intent: >-
  See how you define a result, choose what to compare against, and treat the uncertainty of attribution
  honestly.
signals:
  - States the outcome metric, baseline, observation window, and comparison group
  - Breaks attribution into concrete links and provides evidence for the important links
  - Identifies the weakest evidence, confounders, and alternative explanations
  - >-
    Distinguishes correlation from causation and process metrics from business outcomes, while stating limits
    honestly
