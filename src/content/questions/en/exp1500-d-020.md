title: How can incident actions avoid being abandoned after recovery?
question: "An incident is resolved, and the review lists more than ten actions for monitoring, capacity checks, and documentation; earlier actions were never verified. How would you assign owners and dates and confirm whether improvements worked?"
intent: Assess ownership, prioritization, and effectiveness verification after incidents.
signals:
  - Map actions to causes, failed barriers, and targeted risk, removing duplication or untestable items.
  - >-
    Agree priority, capacity, owners, dates, and escalation with authorized owners rather than merely
    listing tasks.
  - Specify checkable acceptance evidence and use drills, metrics, or later events to test effect.
  - >-
    Track unfinished, ineffective, or risk-shifting actions, clarifying residual risk acceptance and
    review timing.
