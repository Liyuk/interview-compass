title: How can incident actions avoid being abandoned after recovery?
question: "An incident is resolved, and the review lists more than ten actions for monitoring, capacity checks, and documentation; earlier actions were never verified. How would you assign owners and dates and confirm whether improvements worked?"
intent: Assess ownership, prioritization, and effectiveness verification after incidents.
signals:
  - Selects actions by risk reduction, recurrence likelihood, and dependencies.
  - Assigns an owner, deadline, acceptance evidence, and escalation path to each action.
  - Validates controls through drills, metrics, or subsequent incidents.
  - >-
    Reconstruct the goal, prior assumptions, available support, and actual obstacles behind “Retrospective
    actions repeatedly overdue.”
  - Identify which feedback or results changed your understanding and what concrete adjustment followed.
  - >-
    Use later behavior, artifacts, or outcomes to check whether learning, recovery, or self-direction
    persisted.
