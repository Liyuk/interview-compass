title: Why might the same feature fail only on some networks?
question: A feature works on the corporate network and most home networks, but requests often time out on a particular carrier or under poor connectivity; server error rates do not rise at the same time. How would you isolate causes across client, network path, protocol, and retry behavior?

signals:
  - Segments failures by device, network, request phase, and error type.
  - Checks boundaries such as DNS, TLS, proxies, connection reuse, timeout budgets, and request-size limits.
  - Avoids masking faults with unlimited retries and designs reproducible comparisons.
