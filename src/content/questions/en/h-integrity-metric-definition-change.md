title: How do you correct a reported win after the metric definition changes?
question: >-
  Your quarterly report says a target was met. You later discover that the data pipeline changed the
  definition of “active customer” midway through the period; recalculating with the old definition puts the
  result below target. How would you correct the report, inform decision-makers, and repair the process?
intent: >-
  Assess whether when a result favorable to you needs correction, you promptly and transparently fix the
  record and protect later decisions.
signals:
  - Confirms when the definition changed, its impact, and recalculated results while preserving both versions
  - Informs decision-makers who relied on the result and separates facts, unknowns, and possible effects
  - Proposes a corrected report, decision review, and ownership controls against silent definition changes
