title: How do you handle disagreement about intent when the impact is real?
question: >-
  A partner believes you intentionally delayed a project during review; you believe you were raising risks. Both of
  you communicate less as a result. How would you reconstruct facts and repair collaboration?
intent: Assess whether you acknowledge impact without inventing intent and create observable agreements for future work.

  - Gather behavior, context, impact, and each person’s interpretation separately.
  - Acknowledge confirmed impact while keeping intent uncertain.
  - Agree on new communication practices and review them in later work.
