title: When short-term monetization, a strategic direction, and a technical refactor all want resources, how do you build a quantified way to rank them?
question: As the product owner of a business line, you hold at once a short-term monetization request from the business, a strategic direction the boss wants to see, and a technical refactor engineering is asking you to schedule. With resources severely short, how do you build a quantified way of evaluating them to rank these three different kinds of demand?
intent: See whether you can find a comparable basis for different kinds of demand while admitting that some value cannot be fully quantified.
signals:
  - Finds comparable measures for the three kinds, such as benefit, cost, risk, and time window
  - For value that is hard to quantify, such as strategy and technical debt, says how it is estimated through scenarios or risk
  - Says how the result is discussed with decision-makers rather than treating the matrix as the only basis
  - Says how it is reviewed regularly so short-term monetization does not always override the other two
