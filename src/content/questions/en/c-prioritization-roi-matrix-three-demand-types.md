title: "How do you rank short-term revenue, strategic exploration, and technical refactoring?"
question: "The team lacks capacity for all three: a short-term revenue project, strategic exploration, and technical refactoring. What criteria would you use to compare and rank them? How would you explain strategic value or maintenance risk that cannot be measured directly?"
intent: See whether you can find a comparable basis for different kinds of demand while admitting that some value cannot be fully quantified.
signals:
  - Finds comparable measures for the three kinds, such as benefit, cost, risk, and time window
  - For value that is hard to quantify, such as strategy and technical debt, says how it is estimated through scenarios or risk
  - Says how the result is discussed with decision-makers rather than treating the matrix as the only basis
  - Says how it is reviewed regularly so short-term monetization does not always override the other two

followUps:
  - The ranking from the matrix is rejected by the business or the boss. What do you do?
  - The strategic direction has no short-term data behind it. How do you assess it?
  - Sales commitments would take most of this quarter’s engineering capacity, while reliability and data-governance risks are accumulating. How would you assess the cost and risk of postponing those investments?
