title: How do you explain something clearly enough for your manager to see its value or its problem?
question: How do you explain something clearly enough for your manager to see its value or its problem? When they do not take it seriously at first, how do you organize what you say and choose the evidence and the timing?
intent: See whether you can organize information from the listener's side so that they can judge while they still have options.
signals:
  - States what the other person cares about and translates the matter into results or risks they care about
  - Gives the conclusion first, then the evidence, alternatives, and the decision needed
  - Chooses suitable timing and a medium, such as a written note or a small-group conversation
  - When they still do not take it seriously, says how the framing changes rather than repeating the same pitch
