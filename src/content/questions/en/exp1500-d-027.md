title: How much delivery capacity should be invested in expert knowledge transfer?
question: >-
  Only one senior engineer understands a core module. The team wants that person to deliver roadmap work and
  onboard two successors, while the business worries about short-term output. How would you estimate capacity
  for knowledge transfer and its long-term risk and value?
intent: Assess whether capability building is planned as real capacity rather than extra work.
signals:
  - Verify critical module knowledge, nonparallel work, support load, and continuity risk.
  - >-
    Schedule pairing, documentation, drills, and independent handling with acceptance evidence rather
    than treating them as free work alongside delivery.
  - >-
    Compare short-term delivery loss with long-term recovery, parallelism, and continuity benefits,
    stating estimation assumptions.
  - >-
    Agree staged investment, roadmap changes, and backup validation with authorized owners, reviewing
    actual successor capability.
