title: How much delivery capacity should be invested in expert knowledge transfer?
question: Only one senior engineer understands a core module. The team wants that person to deliver roadmap work and onboard two successors, while the business worries about short-term output. How would you estimate capacity for knowledge transfer and its long-term risk and value?
intent: Assess whether capability building is planned as real capacity rather than extra work.
signals:
  - Identifies single-point, support, and continuity risks from employee changes.
  - Plans pairing, documentation, drills, and independent on-call as verifiable work.
  - Compares short-term delivery loss with future response, parallelism, and continuity benefits.
