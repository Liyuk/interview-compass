title: A second-line leader reports only good news
question: >-
  Several teams look healthy on paper, but attrition and delivery risks are never raised. How would you
  diagnose distorted reporting and change the management system?
intent: >-
  Assess how you handle “A second-line leader reports only good news”: clarify goals and constraints, make an
  evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - >-
    Compare reports, delivery records, workload, and when risks first appeared to locate omissions and
    filtering.
  - >-
    Check responses and incentives after escalation, distinguishing missing support, disputed facts, and
    verified concealment.
  - >-
    Establish verifiable independent information sources and escalation paths that connect risk
    discussion to decisions and removing obstacles.
  - >-
    Review earlier risk detection, correction speed, and sustained workload rather than rating leaders by
    bad-news counts.
