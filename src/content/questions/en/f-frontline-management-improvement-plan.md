question: A long-serving employee can no longer keep up with the pace of the business and often spreads negativity on the team. How would you start a performance improvement plan? How do you structure the first conversation, design goals that are specific, measurable, and acceptable to them, and keep the process fair and compliant?
intent: See whether you can make performance improvement a sincere, supported process rather than a formality before an exit, and keep it fair and compliant.
signals:
  - First shows the gap with concrete facts and examples and confirms the person understands, rather than announcing a verdict
  - Designs specific, measurable, time-bound goals and lets the person take part in discussing them
  - Says what support will be given and how progress is checked and recorded
  - Keeps the process sincere so improvement is genuinely possible, while following company policy and working with HR to stay compliant
