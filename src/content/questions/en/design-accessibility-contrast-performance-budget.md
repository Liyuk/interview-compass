title: How do you negotiate a design trade-off when brand style conflicts with readability?
question: >-
  Brand asks for low-contrast text, but users with low vision struggle to read it; increasing contrast changes the
  brand presentation. How would you make and validate the design decision?
intent: Assess whether you treat accessibility as task quality while finding alternatives that preserve brand goals.
signals:
  - Quantify contrast, type size, context, and readability risk for key tasks.
  - Separate brand identity elements from information that must remain readable.
  - Propose alternative palettes and real-user validation instead of relying on taste.
