title: How would you build a mergeable, stable interval index?
question: >-
  The input contains time intervals that may overlap or touch, and callers need to find records covering a
  given instant. Describe or implement a merge and query strategy, including whether endpoints are inclusive.
intent: Assess data-structure choice, boundary contracts, and complexity analysis.
signals:
  - >-
    Defines endpoint inclusion, empty and touching intervals, and original record identities required in
    results.
  - >-
    Compares scans, interval indexes, or source-preserving merges rather than confusing a union with all
    matching records.
  - Explains construction, updates, queries, output-size costs, and space.
  - >-
    Tests nested, overlapping, duplicate, boundary, and large inputs for complete results without
    incorrect merging.
