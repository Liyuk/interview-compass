question: A senior core member has hit a plateau. How do you help them keep growing, and how do you judge whether they have truly hit a ceiling or simply lack a new challenge?
intent: See how you design the next step for someone already strong, and whether you separate a capability ceiling from an environmental one.
signals:

  - Gives a concrete next step, such as wider reach, leading people, or a new area, not only promotion or pay
  - Asks what the person wants rather than deciding for them between the management and specialist tracks
  - Says how it will be judged whether the step works
