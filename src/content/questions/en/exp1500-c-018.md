title: How can quarterly plans stay adjustable when the annual direction is uncertain?
question: >-
  Policy and procurement cycles in a new market are unclear, yet the company must set annual goals and
  budgets. How would you translate long-term direction into quarterly hypotheses, validation milestones, and
  triggers for adjusting the plan?
intent: >-
  Assess how someone links long-term direction, budget commitments, and short-cycle validation under
  uncertainty.
signals:
  - Separates directional goals, testable hypotheses, and fixed commitments.
  - Sequences early milestones that can change investment in the next stage.
  - States plan-review cadence, decision rights, and funding-adjustment triggers.
  - >-
    Define the target users, outcome to change, baseline, and guardrails for “Quarterly goals after annual
    direction shifts.”
  - >-
    Compare alternatives by value, causal evidence, resource needs, and opportunity cost, including what to
    defer.
  - Set signals for continuing, adjusting, expanding, or stopping, and explain how to verify the result.
