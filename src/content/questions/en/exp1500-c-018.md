title: How can quarterly plans stay adjustable when the annual direction is uncertain?
question: >-
  Policy and procurement cycles in a new market are unclear, yet the company must set annual goals and
  budgets. How would you translate long-term direction into quarterly hypotheses, validation milestones, and
  triggers for adjusting the plan?
intent: >-
  Assess how someone links long-term direction, budget commitments, and short-cycle validation under
  uncertainty.
signals:
  - >-
    Separate direction, policy and procurement assumptions, obligations, and adjustable goals rather than
    presenting unknowns as certain returns.
  - Place quarterly tests where they can change the next decision, stating evidence and uncertainty.
  - Compare staged investment, waiting, alternatives, and missed-window costs.
  - >-
    Clarify review cadence, authority over funding and goals, communication, and stop or pivot
    conditions.
