title: How should priorities change when a metric improves while some users are harmed?
question: >-
  A redesign improves average conversion but increases task failure for a constrained user group; expanding
  the sample requires more research time. How would you prioritize the risk and decide whether to pause
  rollout?
intent: Assess value judgment and guardrail use when aggregate benefit conflicts with segment harm.
signals:
  - >-
    Verify average gains and increased failures by segment, task, and severity, checking samples,
    definitions, and alternatives.
  - >-
    Compare aggregate benefit, subgroup harm, validation cost, and reversibility without assuming
    averages offset every risk.
  - Set evidence-based conditions for pausing, limiting rollout, research, or a small continued trial.
  - >-
    Clarify risk acceptance, corrective ownership, and observation, verifying recovery for affected
    users.
