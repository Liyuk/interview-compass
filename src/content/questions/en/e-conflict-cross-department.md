title: How do you coordinate conflicts between departments?
question: How do you coordinate conflicts between departments? When their goals and performance measures differ, how do you find an approach all can accept, and how do you make it stay stable over time?
intent: See how, without a shared superior, you turn disagreement between departments into a common goal and executable rules.
signals:
  - First works out each department's goals, measures, and real concerns, not only the stated positions
  - Finds the genuinely shared goal and uses facts and data to bring the discussion back to it
  - Fixes the agreement into a lasting interface, process, or decision rule, not only personal relationships
  - Says when escalation is needed and how options are presented to higher levels
