title: Manual operational actions are not traceable
question: >-
  Operations staff directly change customer states, and later no one can explain who changed what or why. How
  would you design for efficiency, permission, and auditability?
intent: >-
  Assess how you handle “Manual operational actions are not traceable”: clarify goals and constraints, make an
  evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - Reconstruct the purpose, sensitivity, actors, and downstream effects of customer-state changes.
  - >-
    Separate viewing, editing, approval, and emergency exceptions by necessary authority, retaining
    reasons and before-and-after state.
  - >-
    Compare manual confirmation, controlled tools, and automated validation by efficiency, error, and
    audit cost.
  - >-
    Define detection, reversal or compensation, and review, verifying that records reconstruct real
    actions.
