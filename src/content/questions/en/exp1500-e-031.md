title: How would you negotiate trade-offs among scope, date, and risk?
question: >-
  The date cannot move, but the full solution is no longer feasible. The business wants all features retained.
  How would you propose alternatives and negotiate consequences?
intent: Assess whether constraints, options, and risk acceptance are made explicit.
signals:
  - Compares reduced scope, phasing, and relaxed noncritical requirements.
  - Documents risk acceptance, follow-up commitments, and review criteria.
  - Offers deliverable slices and user impact for each option.
  - Names the risk accepter, follow-up date, and exit criteria.
  - Confirm the real constraint behind the date and cost of moving it.
  - Split the proposal into core outcome, deferred capability, risk, and acceptance criteria.
  - Name who bears extra cost, the decision deadline, and a fallback if criteria are missed.
