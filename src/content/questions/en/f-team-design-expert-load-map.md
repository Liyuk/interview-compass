title: How do you redesign team boundaries when one expert becomes a queue?
question: >-
  Three teams share a senior expert for architecture reviews. Average wait time is over two weeks, and teams
  hesitate to release without approval. How would you determine whether the issue is staffing, concentrated
  knowledge, or decision design, and choose an intervention?
intent: >-
  Assess whether you can trace an individual bottleneck to skill distribution, decision rights, and team
  boundaries instead of simply asking the expert to work more.
signals:
  - >-
    Traces request queues, rework, and release dependencies, distinguishing advice from approval and decision
    ownership
  - >-
    Identifies knowledge to spread through documentation, pairing, or rotation while clarifying judgments that
    still need expert ownership
  - Sets delegation tiers and risk gates, then tracks waiting time, defects, and team independence
