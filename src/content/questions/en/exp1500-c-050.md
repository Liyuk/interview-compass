title: Is expansion sound when one customer contributes too much revenue?
question: >-
  One customer contributes nearly half of product revenue and asks for prioritized custom features; expanding
  similar accounts may grow faster but increase concentration and bargaining risk. How would you assess
  concentration, market opportunity, and product independence?
intent: Assess commercial judgment across customer-portfolio risk and growth opportunity.
signals:
  - Calculates concentration and sensitivity across revenue, renewal, and service cost.
  - Separates reusable market demand from one customer's bespoke requirements.
  - Considers revenue diversification, service boundaries, or staged expansion.
  - Break down revenue, margin, renewal risk, and dedicated service cost by customer
  - Separate reusable needs from one-off customization and identify roadmap opportunity cost
  - Assess loss scenarios for key customers and shifts in bargaining power
  - Propose pricing for customization, product boundaries, or a change in customer mix
