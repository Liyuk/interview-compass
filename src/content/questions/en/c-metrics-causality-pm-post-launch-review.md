title: After a feature launches, how do you judge whether it met its original goal?
question: After a feature launches, how do you judge whether it met its original goal? When do you review it, and what would you decide differently afterwards?
intent: From a product manager's view, see whether the way of checking was set before launch and whether the review really changes later decisions.
signals:
  - Writes down the goal, the way of measuring it, and the observation window before launch, instead of looking for numbers afterwards
  - Separates early post-launch fluctuation from the settled result
  - Says what the review would change, such as continuing to invest, adjusting, or retiring the feature
  - Is candid about cases where the goal was not met
