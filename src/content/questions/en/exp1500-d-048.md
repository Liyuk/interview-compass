title: How would you govern scope changes when the date stays fixed?
question: >-
  Halfway through a project, several stakeholders keep adding important requests while the launch date and
  team capacity stay fixed. How would you establish change decisions and keep scope, risk, and commitments
  transparent?
intent: Assess whether scope, cost, and date constraints are handled through explicit change criteria.
signals:
  - >-
    Require goals, evidence, timing, and necessity for requests, distinguishing clarification from new
    scope.
  - >-
    Check implementation, testing, dependency, operational, and delay costs and compare accepting,
    substituting, deferring, or declining.
  - >-
    Have authorized owners confirm scope, date, and capacity trade-offs rather than accepting every
    request.
  - >-
    Record grounds, commitment changes, and review conditions and notify teams and affected parties
    promptly.
