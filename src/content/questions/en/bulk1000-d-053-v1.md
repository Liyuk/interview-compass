title: Solution proposed before the problem is understood
question: >-
  The requester has prescribed a service rewrite, but incident data points to several possible causes. How
  would you return to problem definition and decide the validation order?
intent: >-
  Assess how you handle “Solution proposed before the problem is understood”: clarify goals and constraints,
  make an evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - >-
    Reconstruct incident types, impact, and checkable data, separating known facts from the hypothesis
    that a rewrite is needed.
  - >-
    Compare fixes, incremental changes, and rewrites by validation cost, risk, and reversibility without
    presuming a rewrite is correct.
  - Test assumptions most likely to change the decision before committing scope and ownership.
  - >-
    Specify evidence for continuing, changing, or stopping and check whether the original problem
    improves.
