intent: From a product manager's view, see how you turn an outcome metric into a plan that can be executed and checked, rather than looking at the number only at the end of the period.

  - Says who owns each link and how often it is checked
  - Writes down in advance what to adjust when it drifts, rather than explaining afterwards
  - Separates what effort alone can reach from what needs product judgment to change
