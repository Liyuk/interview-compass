title: Someone on the team has become tired or complacent. How do you work out why and what to do?
question: Someone on the team has become tired or complacent and their output has clearly dropped. How do you judge whether the cause is workload, lack of growth, personal circumstances, or lost confidence in the team, and what do you do?
intent: See whether you work out the cause before acting rather than labeling the person lazy or unmotivated.
signals:
  - First observes when the change began and what happened around then, and talks with the person, rather than judging by impression
  - Separates overload, lack of growth, personal reasons, and lost confidence in the team
  - Gives different responses for different causes, such as adjusting load, changing the challenge, or offering support
  - Says when expectations and consequences must be made explicit and how it is followed up
