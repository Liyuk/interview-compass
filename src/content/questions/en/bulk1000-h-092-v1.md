  A critical data migration has no owner, and each team says it is outside its scope. How would you make sure the

  No one owns a critical data migration. How do you clarify responsibility? Assess whether you can define the goal

  - >-
    For “No one owns a critical data migration. How do you clarify responsibility?”, inventory the data scope,
    migration steps, rollback conditions, and affected people or systems.
  - Name who executes the migration, who accepts the risk, who verifies data integrity, and who owns it afterward.
  - Explain how you would limit risk while ownership is unclear and pause or roll back if data checks fail.
  - Describe acceptance checks, a recorded handoff, and post-migration monitoring.
