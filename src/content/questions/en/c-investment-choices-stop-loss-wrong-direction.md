title: You discover that a new direction you pushed is wrong. How do you cut your losses?
question: You discover that a new direction you pushed is wrong. How do you cut your losses, how do you judge whether to continue, adjust, or stop, how do you treat sunk cost, and how do you explain it to those involved?
intent: See whether, faced with your own mistake of direction, you can stop in time and honestly without being held back by sunk cost or pride.
signals:
  - Sets in advance the signals that tell whether the direction is right and the conditions for stopping, rather than finding reasons afterwards
  - Separates money already spent from the expected return of further investment
  - Tells those involved plainly about the mistake and the plan to cut losses
  - Says what is kept after stopping, such as lessons, reusable parts, and an account to the team
