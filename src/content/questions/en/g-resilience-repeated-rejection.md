title: How do you recover judgment after repeated rejection instead of simply pushing harder?
question: >-
  Two proposals you made have been rejected by key decision-makers, and the team is questioning your judgment.
  How would you review both outcomes, decide whether the problem is still worth pursuing, and restore
  confidence in the next step?
intent: >-
  Assess whether after repeated setbacks you can separate the proposal, the problem’s value, and
  self-evaluation, then adjust based on evidence.
signals:
  - Reconstructs the reasons for rejection, available evidence, and assumptions they may have missed
  - Distinguishes a valuable problem with a poor solution, poor timing, or insufficient value
  - Proposes a next validation step or a clear stop and explains the learning to the team
  - Separate criticism of the proposal from personal worth and restore a workable cadence.
  - Verify the critique and identify what you should revise.
  - Agree on revision, review timing, and needed support with stakeholders.
