title: Two people on your team are in conflict. How do you judge what is going on and what to do?
question: Two people on your team are in conflict. How do you judge what is going on? How do you separate fact, position, and emotion, and when do you decide directly versus let them settle it themselves?
intent: See how you find the real cause of a conflict without taking sides and choose a fitting level of intervention.
signals:
  - Hears each side separately and separates facts, each person's goals, and emotion rather than taking sides first
  - Cross-checks with verifiable information and does not judge by impression or relationship
  - Says when to rule directly and when to let the two resolve it, with the basis
  - Follows up afterwards on whether the relationship recovered, rather than stopping once things calm down
