intent: >-
  Explore whether your motivations and conditions are clear so both sides can tell if expectations are compatible; this
  records each side's terms rather than ranking anyone.
signals:
  - "Distinguish non-negotiable conditions from negotiable ones and explain why they matter."
  - "Turn growth goals into specific capabilities, responsibility scope, and verifiable opportunities."
  - "Use concrete examples to check the role’s actual work style, decision rights, and support."
  - "Explain how to weigh a mismatch and when to revisit either side’s expectations."
