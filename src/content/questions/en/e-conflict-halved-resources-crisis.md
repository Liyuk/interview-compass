title: Online business metrics are cut in half and resources are halved, and product, operations, and engineering must weather it together. How do you collaborate instead of passing the blame?
question: Suppose online business metrics are cut in half and resources are halved, and product, operations, and engineering must get through the crisis together. How do you work with the other two functions in this situation to decide what to protect, what to cut, and how to share the burden, rather than pushing blame around?
intent: See how, in a crisis with sharply reduced resources, you build shared judgment with other functions and share the trade-offs openly.
signals:
  - First aligns on the facts, such as why the metrics fell and the real extent and time window of the reduction in resources
  - Lists with the other functions what must be protected and what can be paused and says the basis
  - Makes each function's costs and concessions open so no side carries it alone
  - Agrees checkpoints and conditions for adjusting rather than deciding once and never revisiting
