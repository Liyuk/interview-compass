title: Manage an early mismatch between interview evidence and actual work
question: >-
  One month after joining, a new hire’s delivery quality is well below the capability shown in
  interviews, but task context and onboarding support have also been incomplete. How would you
  distinguish selection, onboarding, and management issues and set next steps?
intent: Examines evidence-based diagnosis instead of quickly blaming the hire.
signals:
  - Compares role expectations, actual assignments, and the scope of interview evidence.
  - Checks whether tools, context, feedback, and support were available.
  - Sets clear expectations, short feedback loops, and a fair observation window.
