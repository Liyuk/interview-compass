title: How do you keep the core talent on your team?
question: How do you keep the core talent on your team? How do you notice early that someone wants to leave, and when someone really does, how do you keep the team from being hollowed out?
intent: See whether you treat retention as ongoing management work and also reduce dependence on any one person.
signals:
  - Says how to notice early signs of discontent or intent to leave, instead of waiting for a resignation
  - Understands whether the person cares most about growth, recognition, pay, or the work itself, and acts accordingly
  - Also reduces dependence on one person, for example through knowledge capture and backups
  - Is candid about cases where retention failed and says how a good handover was arranged
