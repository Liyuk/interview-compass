title: Tell me about the piece of work you are proudest of. Why is it the best?
question: Tell me about the piece of work you are proudest of. Why is it the best, what yardstick do you measure it by, and which parts were your own contribution?
intent: See what standard of "good" you hold, and whether you can separate your own contribution from the team's result.
signals:
  - Gives the yardstick for "best" first, not only the feeling
  - Describes your own judgment and actions, and separates the team's contribution
  - Names the cost paid for it, or what was given up
