title: Status reporting disconnected from actual risk
question: >-
  A project stays green until a critical data migration is found untested just before launch. How would you
  improve status signals and decision cadence?

  Assess how you handle “Status reporting disconnected from actual risk”: clarify goals and constraints, make
  an evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - >-
    Turn “Status reporting disconnected from actual risk” into an acceptable outcome, work items, owners, and
    prerequisites.
  - >-
    Explain how capacity, critical paths, risks, and conflicting commitments become visible and trigger
    replanning or escalation.
  - Define milestones, completion evidence, acceptance gates, and conditions for handoff to ongoing owners.
  - Uses verifiable milestones and outcome evidence instead of subjective completion percentages.
  - Separates known facts, forecast ranges, risks, and assumptions.
  - States who needs to decide what by when and the impact of no decision.
  - Separates current delivery state, forecast date, and forecast confidence.
  - Uses milestone drift, unconfirmed dependencies, and critical-path risk as warning signals.
  - Defines escalation triggers, decision deadlines, and ownership for correcting status.
  - Make critical path, dependencies, and decision latency observable.
  - Separate current state, forecast, and confidence.
  - Set red-status triggers and evidence for clearing them.
