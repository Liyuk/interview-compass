title: What does it mean when users repeatedly bypass the main flow?
question: >-
  Analytics show target users skipping the product's main flow and using exports, spreadsheets, or manual
  coordination instead; the team believes this is a user-habit problem. How would you decide whether the
  product conflicts with real work practices?
intent: "Assess whether the respondent investigates the user task and constraints behind workarounds before deciding whether to repair, change process, or redesign the product model."
signals:
  - "Observes the complete task across product and offline tools, identifying the trigger, roles, rules, and cost of each workaround."
  - "Checks whether permissions, missing capabilities, collaboration, or migration friction cause the bypass and verifies claims with multiple roles."
  - "Compares a product fix, process change, and domain-model redesign by user outcome, effort, and risk."
  - "Tests a targeted change and measures task completion, errors, switching cost, and remaining offline work."
