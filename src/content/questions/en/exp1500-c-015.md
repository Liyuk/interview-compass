title: Which commitments should change when strategic priorities shift mid-quarter?
question: A new market opportunity emerges mid-quarter and leadership wants an immediate pivot; customer promises, team goals, and partner schedules have already been published. How would you compare the opportunity window with the cost of breaking commitments and decide what to keep, reduce, or renegotiate?
intent: Assess trade-off judgment among shifting priorities, commitments, trust, and opportunity cost.
signals:
  - Distinguishes adjustable internal goals from external commitments that cannot be changed unilaterally.
  - Evaluates timing, switching cost, affected parties, and reversibility.
  - Sets a transparent renegotiation sequence and a new decision baseline.
