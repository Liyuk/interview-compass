title: How do you recover momentum and learn after a proposal is rejected publicly?
question: >-
  A cross-functional review finds a key assumption in your proposal was wrong. The notes are visible to everyone,
  and delivery is due next week. How would you handle emotion, facts, and next steps?

signals:
  - Separate criticism of the proposal from personal worth and restore a workable cadence.
  - Verify the critique and identify what you should revise.
  - Agree on revision, review timing, and needed support with stakeholders.
