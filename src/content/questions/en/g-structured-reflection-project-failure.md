title: Tell me about a project that did not succeed. Why did it fail?
question: Tell me about a project that did not succeed. Why did it fail, which causes were direct and which more fundamental, and how much of it related to your own judgment?
intent: See your ability to summarize, in particular whether you separate direct causes from fundamental ones without blaming everything outside or taking everything on yourself.
signals:
  - Separates direct causes from more fundamental ones, with the basis for each
  - Points to the part that relates to your own judgment, rather than putting it all outside
  - Says where the signs of failure were and whether they could have been seen earlier
  - Says which conclusions are fact and which are still conjecture
