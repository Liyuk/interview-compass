title: A critical dependency has no owner or cannot enter the schedule
question: >-
  A project depends on another team for an interface or data contract, but there is no clear delivery owner,
  plans keep changing, and the work may not fit next quarter’s schedule. How would you establish a commitment,
  prepare alternatives, manage critical-path risk, and decide when to escalate or change scope?
intent: >-
  Assesses whether you can turn an external dependency into a commitment with an owner, date, acceptance
  criteria, and decision path, while surfacing alternatives before it fails.
signals:
  - Confirms the deliverable, owner, capacity, acceptance criteria, and latest needed date
  - Separates controllable from external dependencies and tracks critical-path and commitment changes
  - Proposes alternatives and compares cost, scope, and quality risks
  - Defines escalation triggers, decision makers, and when commitments must be reset
