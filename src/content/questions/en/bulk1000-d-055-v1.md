title: A critical dependency has no owner or cannot enter the schedule
question: >-
  A project depends on another team for an interface or data contract, but there is no clear delivery owner,
  plans keep changing, and the work may not fit next quarter’s schedule. How would you establish a commitment,
  prepare alternatives, manage critical-path risk, and decide when to escalate or change scope?
intent: >-
  Assess how you handle “A critical dependency has no owner or cannot enter the schedule”: clarify goals and
  constraints, make an evidence-based judgment, and explain how you would verify outcomes and trade-offs.
signals:
  - >-
    Confirm interface contracts, accountable owners, capacity, and the latest confirmation date with
    relevant owners without committing on another team’s behalf.
  - >-
    Map interface changes and delays to the critical path, acceptance, and next-quarter plans, making
    unresolved assumptions explicit.
  - Compare alternate interfaces, mocks, resequencing, smaller scope, or delay by cost and risk.
  - Agree escalation and recommitment triggers, decision rights, and stakeholders to notify.
