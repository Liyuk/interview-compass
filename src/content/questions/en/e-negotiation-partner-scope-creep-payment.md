title: How do you negotiate when a partner adds out-of-scope work but rejects a price change?

  A partner adds operational and reporting requests every week, calling them included services. Team capacity is
  affecting other customers. How would you establish facts and negotiate scope or service level?
intent: Assess whether you use contract, effort, service impact, and alternatives to negotiate commercially.

  - Compare contract scope with added requests and their cost.
  - Assess effects of each option on customer outcomes and other customers.
  - Offer paid work, limits, self-service, or formal change options.
