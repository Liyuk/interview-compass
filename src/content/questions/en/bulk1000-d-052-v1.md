title: 'Decomposing an ambiguous commitment into verifiable outcomes: A critical dependency slips by one cycle'
question: >-
  The business asks to “improve customer experience” without a clear pain point or deadline. How would you
  decompose it into verifiable problems, workstreams, and milestones?

  Follow-up: If a critical dependency slips by one cycle, how would you re-sequence the critical path, reset
  commitments, and reduce waiting?
intent: "Assess whether you translate an ambiguous experience goal into a verifiable user outcome, delivery steps, dependencies, and ownership, then update commitments when evidence is missing or a dependency slips."
signals: ["Clarify user, blocked task, baseline, horizon, and assumptions rather than turning “improve experience” directly into a feature list.", "Split discovery, validation, delivery, and outcome review into stages with owners, dependencies, capacity, and acceptance evidence.", "When a key dependency slips, compare sequence, scope, and date alternatives, identifying who can recommit.", "Set checkpoints, escalation, and stop signals and validate user outcomes rather than task completion."]
anchors:
  notPassing: "Creates a feature list without a user outcome or baseline."

  excellent: "Handles uncertainty and critical-path changes, clarifies recommit authority, and validates the outcome."
  exceptional: "Uses reversible investment stages, quantifies commitment ranges and dependency risk, and refuses false precision when evidence is insufficient."
