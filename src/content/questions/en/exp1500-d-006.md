title: What if several teams schedule the same domain expert as critical-path capacity?
question: >-
  Several teams list the same domain expert as a critical-path resource and each plan assumes full-time
  support. How would you establish capacity facts, compare dependency risk, and negotiate a deliverable
  arrangement?
intent: Assess capacity modeling and cross-team commitment negotiation for scarce shared resources.
signals:
  - Verifies actual availability, task types, and context-switching cost.
  - Compares project delay, substitute capability, and the value of transferring knowledge.
  - Creates a commitment with priority, capacity limits, escalation rules, and a review date.
  - 'A shared expert promised to several projects: make the capacity and priority decision explicit.'
  - Compare critical paths, delay impact, alternate reviewers, staged reviews, and scope changes.
  - State who can reset commitments and how deferred risks will be communicated.
  - Check that capacity, owners, and milestones in the revised plan are feasible.
  - >-
    Clarifies the facts, stakeholders, and ownership boundaries involved in priority and risk transparency for
    scarce shared expertise.
