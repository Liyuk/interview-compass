title: What if several teams schedule the same domain expert as critical-path capacity?
question: >-
  Several teams list the same domain expert as a critical-path resource and each plan assumes full-time
  support. How would you establish capacity facts, compare dependency risk, and negotiate a deliverable
  arrangement?
intent: Assess capacity modeling and cross-team commitment negotiation for scarce shared resources.
signals:
  - >-
    Verify expert availability, support tasks, and context switching, exposing capacity double-counted
    across plans.
  - >-
    Compare critical paths, delay impact, substitutes, staged reviews, and knowledge transfer by cost and
    benefit.
  - >-
    Agree priorities, capacity limits, escalation, and review dates with authorized owners rather than
    unilaterally resetting other teams’ commitments.
  - Check that milestones, owners, and handoffs are feasible and communicate deferred work and risk.
