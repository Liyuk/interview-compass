title: The boss says acquisition cost is high and retention is poor. How do you translate that into goals the technical team can tackle?
question: The boss raises a business pain, such as high acquisition cost and poor retention. How do you translate it into goals the technical team can tackle, and show how technical means act on those business metrics?
intent: See whether you can bridge business and technology, turning a business problem into executable technical goals and showing the causality.
signals:
  - First works out where the pain really comes from and breaks it into specific stages and metrics
  - Maps the stages to levers technology can change, such as load speed, recall accuracy, and stability
  - Says how each technical goal moves the business metric and how that is verified
  - Admits which business problems technology cannot solve and says so frankly
