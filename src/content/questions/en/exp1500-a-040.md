title: How can instructions inside retrieved documents be prevented from granting tool access?
question: >-
  An AI assistant reads user-uploaded documents that may tell it to ignore existing rules and call external
  tools. How would you distinguish data from instructions, constrain tool permissions, and verify the
  safeguards?
intent: Assess threat modeling and defense in depth against prompt injection.

    external sends.
  - >-
    Compares isolation, least privilege, confirmation, and escalation without claiming prompt wording is
    complete protection.
  - Tests injection, unauthorized tools, and exfiltration with policy evidence and stated residual risk.
