title: How can evaluation be reproducible when data contains personal information?
question: >-
  The team needs real user conversations to reproduce model failures, but logs contain personal and sensitive
  business content. How would you minimize collection, redact data, authorize access, and set retention while
  preserving debugging value?
intent: Assess governance trade-offs between AI quality evaluation and privacy protection.
signals:
  - Identifies the fields needed for reproduction and avoids retaining full raw text by default.
  - Explains distinct roles and limits of de-identification, redaction, encryption, and access control.
  - Defines purpose limitation, retention, deletion verification, and audits of exceptional access.
  - Separate required telemetry from raw content by purpose.
  - Define redaction, access controls, and retention at ingestion.
  - Verify logging neither leaks data nor prevents diagnosis.
