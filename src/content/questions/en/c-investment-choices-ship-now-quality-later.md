question: The boss or the business side squeezes the schedule and says "ship first, messy code is fine". As the owner, how do you respond? Which quality can give way and which cannot, and how do you make the concession open and time-limited rather than quietly becoming permanent debt?
intent: See how you make open quality trade-offs under pressure, hold the lines that truly matter, and give each concession a plan to repay it.
signals:
  - Separates quality that can give way from lines that cannot, such as data correctness, security, and rollback
  - Makes the trade-off open, telling decision-makers the cost and risk of the concession instead of absorbing it privately
  - Records the concession and agrees when it will be repaid, rather than slipping repayment into later sprints unseen
  - Says how it is checked that the debt is really repaid
