# Guide

Interview Compass is a heuristic question library. Each prompt explains what capability it is meant to explore, which topics it connects to, and how nearby questions differ. It does not provide model answers.

## How questions are organized

Explore from the question index, a topic, or a role. Technical capability, domain modeling, engineering delivery, project and organization management, personal growth, and product outcomes can be browsed across dimensions.

## How to use a question

Read the prompt, then its intent to understand why it is asked. Follow the related topics, and compare adjacent prompts to understand their boundaries. Intent notes help readers understand a question; they are not candidate scoring criteria.

## Content principles

- Prompts stay open-ended and include no model answers, sample responses, or hiring verdicts.
- Every published question has Simplified Chinese and English versions.
- Classification describes relevant capabilities and contexts; a question may connect to multiple topics and roles.
- Sources are linked and attributed. Unverified origins are not invented.
