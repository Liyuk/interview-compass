# Evidence rubric used by Interview Compass Coach

Use the question's own reference anchors first. The generic meanings below are orientation only; the role, target stage, responsibility scope, and question-specific evidence determine what the anchor means in context.

| Score | Label | Evidence reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Not meeting the bar | Basic concepts, facts, or account of personal work cannot be established after fair clarification. A verified deliberate fabrication is disqualifying; an inconsistency alone is not evidence of fabrication. |
| 2 | Meets the bar | The person explains the core idea and a workable approach, and can substantiate the relevant work or reasoning expected at this scope. |
| 3 | Strong | The answer shows structured reflection and above-expected practice, such as a useful insight, strong causal reasoning, or a repeatable improvement. |
| 4 | Exceptional | Rare evidence of industry-leading work for the relevant scope, with measurable results that can be verified and credibly attributed to the person's decisions and actions. |

Score only when there is enough evidence. Record the response, missing context, and inference separately. Ask follow-ups before treating a gap as a negative finding. Do not average dimensions into a person-level score. In most interviews, 2 is the expected bar; 3 may appear in one or two relevant dimensions; 4 should be uncommon. These anchors are heuristics, not validated psychometrics or employment recommendations.
