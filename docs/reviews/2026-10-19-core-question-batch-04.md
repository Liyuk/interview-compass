## Track and applicability review

Career tracks and responsibility scopes were narrowed to at most two values per core question based on decision authority and evidence scope. Examples: individual career choices remain individual-contributor prompts; recruiting calibration and cross-team leadership use manager tracks; senior portfolio decisions retain team/senior-manager scope. Each changed applicability fingerprint was re-reviewed against the previous approved profile and current role/track suggestions. Only previously reviewed combinations still in scope were retained; no role/stage combination was added. Coverage remains 1,334 reviewed, 0 pending, 0 empty reviewed profiles.

## Editorial examples

- **Risk communication under uncertainty:** separates facts, assumptions, unknowns, mitigations, and decision thresholds instead of asking for a premature probability.
- **Interview feedback calibration:** requires role evidence and observation-gap analysis, with targeted follow-up rather than score averaging or seniority voting.
- **Fatigue and declining output:** distinguishes workload, growth, personal boundaries, confidence, and expectations before choosing support or performance action.
- **New-hire ramp:** replaces a fixed-time task list with progressively delegated judgment and evidence for independent routine and exception handling.
